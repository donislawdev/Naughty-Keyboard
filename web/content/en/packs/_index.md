A pack is a set of values chosen for one kind of field or one kind of mistake, in the order a tester meets them: the strongest first. Each value carries a sentence about what it usually breaks, and why, and one about what a correct application does. That sentence is what makes a catalogue worth more than a list of strings.

The packs are free to use under CC BY 4.0, in the palette, through `nkb emit` in a script, or as the files they are in the repository.
